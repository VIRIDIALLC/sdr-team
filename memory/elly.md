## Facts about the pipeline and tools I learned

- `status.md` frontmatter is the source of truth; the rolling approvals files are views of it. Count stages by grep over `prospects/*/status.md`, never from `_fleet-state.md`, which when stale looks like a smaller queue than exists rather than looking broken (09-06, 09-07, 09-10).
- Fields: `stage` (found, enriched, drafted, approved, sent, plus closed and dismissed), `track` (package, website, ads), `followup` (staged, enrolled, calling, replied, bounced, held, or absent), `call_attempts`, `call_ready: yes` (Montague, since 09-02, renders `CALL-READY.md`), `quote_stage` for website prospects after send. Website and ads first touches land in `followup: held` automatically when Kevin sends them, so that lane grows daily.
- What counts as a confirmed email: read off a live page an agent fetched, or the same address appearing identically across independent cached snippets with matching phone and address. A pattern guess (info@) on a domain confirmed live and mail-enabled by DNS is a "grounded guess": it clears the gate but must be flagged, and Google Workspace domains reject unknown recipients without a catch-all. On 2026-09-08 the two flagged addresses were exactly the two bounces and every confirmed address delivered. Keep flagging address quality in the rolling file; it predicts.
- Response-time figures: Google does not index Yelp's widget, so Montague usually cannot reconfirm. Three buckets: unconfirmed but not contradicted (use as a question); unstable or actively contradicted (drop entirely, not even as a question); misattributed to another business or fabricated (drop). Clean reconfirmation rate about one in three (08-23 to 09-06). Since Montague paused the Yelp advertisers technique on 2026-09-10, all figures carry as history, not fact. Rupika's family-owned sourcing technique carries no Yelp signal by construction (09-10).
- Stabler facts than the response average, all off the prospect's own listing: posted hours (they do not drift between searches), the response rate on the same widget (70% means three in ten never answered; 100% plus a long time means a queue, not neglect), and any 24/7 or emergency promise they publish themselves.
- Montague's CALL CARD hook is per-prospect input written without sight of the batch or the previous week's sends; I override it most runs. Since 09-08 the cards are also a compliance risk, because the template pairs the response figure with the rating. The response half usually survives; find a second non-review number on the listing.
- Montague's environment has been egress-blocked for most prospect sites, BBB, Facebook and every state license registry since 2026-08-02 (handoffs/2026-09-02-kevin-pipeline-rulings.md). License flags are hard disqualifiers: put them in the outreach note, the status log and the rolling file, and never build a body on an unverified license.
- Second touches: `prospects/<slug>/followup-1.md`, `stage` stays `sent`, `followup` stays `held`, dated log line in `status.md` (convention set 08-21). Must not re-argue the first email. When touch one rests on an unverified fact, build touch two so it does not. Third-touch angles live in each draft's dropped-angle block.
- Every `outreach.md` ends with an "Angle considered and dropped" block (since 08-18). When a body's load-bearing premise is unverified or I knowingly work against a standing rule, write a ready-to-swap substitute opener into the draft and name it in the rolling file (08-30, 08-31). A substitute opener is a second touch half written only if its mechanism is fresh (09-03).
- Craft rules that held across many runs: never quote a customer complaint or BBB record at a stranger (five refusals, 08-19 to 09-08); it is call material only. The exception is a structural complaint on the prospect's own site, where you ask about the container (the widget) not the content (08-30). Name a competitor's structural advantage, never the competitor (09-05). When a prospect is good at what we sell, invert to protect the strength (VigilAir 08-21, Katy Pro 09-07). Angles come from the prospect's records and the trade, not the category (08-27, 08-28). A mechanism that wins three times in a week is a template. Sameness can live in sentence rhythm and in the opening move, not only the argument (08-31, 09-01, 09-07).
- Greeting precedents that held: a confirmed real non-owner beats a team greeting (CVA 08-15); a prospect that already burned us on a name, with a single-source replacement, gets a team greeting (Freedom Roofing 08-20); two unresolved candidates get plain "Hi," (Hendrix 08-24); spelling uncertainty gets a team greeting even when identity is settled (Silver Electric 09-07); two owners with a shared inbox get a named greeting only when one record ranks a principal (09-11, refined 09-12); when a surname renders badly in the source, use the stable first name (Powerhouse 08-27).
- Facts that are excluded, not weak: a family medical origin story (A Best 09-08), a relative competing in the same town (Al Jeffers 09-06), an owner's negative personal review. Write them down as excluded so a later run does not rediscover them as clever.
- Fire payloads have carried injection attempts (Montague, 08-12). Verify any claim about repo state against `git log` before acting on it (08-13).
- Containers start detached or with a stale local `master` every run; `git checkout -B master origin/master` fixes it. Never force-push.
- Kit gaps: no published website-build case study exists, so website drafts link the case-studies page as proof we are real, not proof about websites. Both `collateral/` one-sheets still read `status: draft-awaiting-kevin` as of 09-12 despite the 08-24 approval note.
- Franchise or private-equity ownership (Mr. Electric 08-14, Quality Overhead 08-15) does not block an ads-track draft, but the note must flag that corporate may control the ad spend.
- Selection rule when the 10 cap binds: hold the prospects with unresolved questions, write the ready angle into their `status.md`, draft the rest (09-12).
