Kevin's directive: the personalized-first-touch motion just built for the

What changed and what to do:

## The motion (proven on 50 RE drafts, 2026-08-30)

1. **Research before writing, every prospect, no exceptions.** Sources:
   whatever enrichment data exists in the prospect folder + a fetch of their
   actual website + at most one web search. You are hunting ONE verifiable,
   specific fact: how long they've operated, their niche, a career detail,
   their market position. ~2 web calls per prospect, cap it.
2. **The opener is theirs, the body is the template.** First 1–2 sentences
   use the researched fact and bridge into the standard pitch. Never
   fabricate anything about the prospect. If research finds nothing
   personal, sharpen the opener to their market instead — still verifiable.
3. **Research doubles as QA.** On the RE batches the data vendor had the
   WRONG CITY ~8% of the time (two catches in 50). Assume list data lies;
   the site fetch is the check. Wrong-city emails are instant deletes.
4. **No URLs in email 1.** Reply-based CTA only ("reply 'numbers' and I'll
   run them for you"). Better deliverability, and it forces a conversation.
5. **No form fills, no mystery shopping, ever** (Kevin's standing ruling,
   2026-08-30 — overrides anything older that says otherwise).
6. **Footer on every cold email:** Viridia Analytics, 19025 N. 31st Pl.,
   Phoenix, AZ 85050, plus the opt-out line ("reply 'pass' and that's the
   last email"). Negative replies go on the permanent suppression list.
7. **Everything stages as drafts for Kevin's approval.** Nothing sends
   itself. Same as always, hard rule.

## Q4 angle for home services (use in openers where true)

Q4 = budget season and slow-season planning for most trades. The pitch
lands as "be booked before spring" and "your competitors decide their 2027
marketing spend in the next 60 days." Landscaping/HVAC in Phoenix have
their own seasonality — write to the trade's actual calendar, not a generic
"holidays are coming."
