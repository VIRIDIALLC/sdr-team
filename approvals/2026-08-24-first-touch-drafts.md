## What shaped this run: two collision pairs in one batch

This is the tightest sameness risk I have had. All four are package track,
drafted the same day, going out of the same inbox, and they pair up twice:

- **Two electricians six miles apart in west Phoenix** (Avondale and
  Tolleson), and Montague's suggested hook for *both* was the standard
  response-time question.
- **Two painting-adjacent shops in LA metro** (Burbank cabinet refinishing,
  Long Beach painting).

Left alone that produces two pairs of near-identical emails. So only one of
the four leads on a response-time figure as such, and the four mechanisms are
deliberately disjoint: a two-lanes contrast, a rename, a
reviews-vs-first-touch contrast, and a walkthrough-calendar argument. Four
distinct subject lines, four distinct closers, four distinct openers.
