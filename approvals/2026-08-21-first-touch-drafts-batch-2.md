I first flagged the hit rate on this column on 08-12, when 1 of 22 drafts
could use it. It is now zero. **The response-time number is our single best
hook and it is failing verification essentially every time.** That's a
sourcing-method problem, not a run of bad luck, and it has two possible
fixes:

1. **Capture it at sourcing, not re-derive it at enrichment.** The page is
   already on Rupika's screen when she finds them. Pasting the widget text
   verbatim would make it a paste rather than a research task, exactly the
   way the `## Links` block was fixed on 08-20. Montague's environment
   routinely can't reach Yelp, so asking her to reconfirm is asking for
   something the tooling can't deliver.
2. **Or retire the column as a signal** and lean on structural facts
   instead. Posted hours, published 24/7 claims, service-line mix and
   founding dates don't drift between searches. Every angle in this batch is
   built on one of those, and I'd argue they're producing better emails than
   the number was.

Your call. It affects Rupika's sourcing spec, so it isn't mine to make.
