## The mechanism behind each one

Seven emails leaving one inbox on one day is the worst sameness risk this seat
gets, and five of the seven are package track. Each is built on a different
mechanism on purpose:

- **Total Restoration** — two arrival paths. The 2am burst pipe is a phone call
  and they are genuinely good at it. The ceiling that stained last Tuesday is a
  message from somebody comparing three companies from the couch. The email
  concedes the first outright so nothing in it says she is bad at her own trade.
- **Kolor Pros** — competitor structure. The franchises in the same Austin
  searches are not better painters, they just have somebody whose whole job is
  picking up. That is the one advantage that has nothing to do with painting and
  the one we can hand her in a week.
- **Dos Lunas** — the shortlist closes before he answers. Design-build work
  (patio covers, stamped concrete, pergolas) is planned and comparison-shopped,
  so the buyer messages four landscapers in one sitting on a weekend. He is not
  losing to a competitor, he is absent from a list that fills in an afternoon.
- **4 Seasons Pest** — urgency decay, which is specific to this trade. Somebody
  who just found mice wants it gone today and is not lining up bids. The job
  isn't lost on price or reviews, it's lost to whoever answered.
- **JE & BC Electric** — their best credential is filed somewhere invisible.
  BuildZoom score 99, top 15% of about 13,069 NH contractors, 36 permits pulled
  2021 to 2023. Real, verified, and no homeowner has ever heard of BuildZoom.
- **Clean Earth** — the missing catalog. Mowing customers cannot see that he
  also does fences, tree removal and turf, because Facebook shows only the last
  thing posted, so the cheapest repeat work in the trade walks to a stranger.
- **Green Texas HVAC** — before reviews exist, speed is the only proof a listing
  has. Built entirely on two facts they published themselves, which matters
