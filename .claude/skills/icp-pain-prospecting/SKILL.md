# ICP + pain prospecting — find the businesses that are visibly bleeding leads

The Complete System ($1,197/mo) sells itself to a home-service owner who is losing leads to
missed calls, slow follow-up, and no system. You can't query "missed calls" in a database — but
the pain leaves **observable footprints**. Your job: find businesses showing those footprints, so
outreach lands on someone who already feels it. A prospect with a named, specific pain converts;
a generic "home-service business" does not.

## Who (the ICP)
Home/field-service SMB — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, restoration, pest control,
landscaping, painting, garage doors, remodeling. **Owner-operated or owner + a small team**, the
owner still doing sales/ops while on the tools. **Target markets: Phoenix and Los Angeles metros** —
that's where the primary hunt for actual (Complete System, recurring) clients runs. Work those two
markets; don't spread nationwide. See `prospects/_icp-home-service.md` for the full pain / emotion /
money / language picture.

## The pain signals — this is the whole game
A PRIME Complete System fit shows **2+ of these** (more signals = hotter lead):
1. **Paying for leads but slow to respond.** A Yelp/Google advertiser with a stated response time
   **over 1 hour**, or reviews saying "never called me back," "took days." They're buying leads and
   losing them — the sharpest signal there is.
2. **No fast way to book or reach them.** No online booking/scheduling, no "text us"/live chat, no
   after-hours option. If *you* can't easily book them, neither can their customers.
3. **Reviews that show the leak.** Unanswered reviews (no owner responses = no reputation system),
   or complaints about missed calls / no-shows / slow response. Few reviews despite years in
   business = leads not converting.
4. **Running ads with no capture.** Active Yelp/Google/Meta ads but nothing behind them (no chat
   widget, no instant response) = paying for traffic that leaks out.
5. **No or bad website.** No site, or an old/broken/non-mobile one with no lead capture — leads
   have nowhere to land.
6. **Solo/small and always in the field.** One or two-person shop, owner clearly on the tools —
   physically can't answer every call.

## How to find them
- `[niche] yelp advertisers in [market]` → look for a stated response time **> 1 hour** (the money signal).
- `[niche] [city]` on Google/Maps → scan for: no booking link, unanswered reviews, complaints about
  calls/response, missing/old website.
- Check their Google Business Profile + Yelp: response rate, review recency, "request a quote" speed.

## Capture the WHY, every time
In each `brief.md`, write the **exact signal(s) you observed** — the specific reason this business
is bleeding leads — so Montague/Elly can open outreach with it ("saw your Yelp says ~3hr response
time…"). That named pain is what makes the message land.

## The bar (see craft-operating-system)
Never queue a prospect you can't name a real pain signal for. **Quality over volume** — 5
businesses you can prove are losing leads beat 50 random ones. No signal = not a prospect yet.
