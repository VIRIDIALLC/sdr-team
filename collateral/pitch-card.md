# THE PITCH CARD — keep this on screen for every dial

*(One screen. Kevin's own ruling 2026-08-21: "I need to STFU when pitching —
when they want to sign up, that's my problem." The card's whole job is making
that automatic.)*

## 1 · THE STING (pick one, then ask a question and stop)

- "Quick question — when someone calls you while you're on a job, what
  happens to that call?" *(let them say "voicemail")* → "Right. And where do
  you think they call next?"
- "You ever gotten a review that says 'never called me back' or 'never
  showed'? ... You know that wasn't the work — that was the phone."
- "You're paying for leads, right — Yelp, Google? So every missed call is a
  lead you paid for twice. Once to get it, once to lose it."

**Rule: the sting is a QUESTION, not a speech. Their answer does the selling.**

## 2 · QUALIFY (their numbers, not your pitch)

- "How many calls or leads a week you getting?" · "How many you figure slip?"
- "What's an average job worth to you?" → **say the math OUT LOUD:**
  "So ~[N] missed × $[Y] — that's ~$[Z] a month walking to the next guy."
- "You the one who'd make this call?" · "Could you start this month?"
- ☠️ Disqualify without mercy: no lead volume, no budget, not the decision
  maker, wants it free. Thank them, next dial.

## 3 · THE PITCH (three sentences. Count them.)

"One system: every call, text, and Yelp lead answered and booked in seconds,
24/7 — you keep swinging the hammer. It's $1,197 a month, month to month.
And here's the part nobody else will say: if we don't respond to every missed
call in your first 14 days, the month is free — all of it back."

## 4 · THE CLOSE — then SHUT UP

> **"To lock one of this month's five spots it's $497 to get you set up, plus
> your first month, fully guaranteed. Want me to get you started?"**

**■ SAY NOTHING. Whoever talks next loses. Silence is them deciding YES —
every word you add gives them a reason to decide later.**

- **They say yes →** "Great — texting you the link right now." LOGISTICS
  ONLY from here. Never re-sell after yes; re-selling reopens the decision.
- **Buying signals mid-pitch** ("how's setup work?" "how fast?") **→ that IS
  the yes forming. Skip to the close line immediately.** Do not finish
  your pitch. The pitch is done when they lean in, not when you're done.
- **They stall →** "Totally fair. What's the one thing giving you pause?"
  Answer THAT in one sentence, then the close line again. Once.

## 5 · OBJECTIONS — one sentence each, then back to the close

- **"Robot?"** → "It answers and texts back in seconds so a human takes it
  from there — the alternative is voicemail, and voicemail is zero."
- **"Burned by agencies."** → "That's why it's month to month and the first
  month's guaranteed — I only keep you if it works."
- **"Expensive."** → "It's one saved job. What was your average job again?"
- **"Not techy."** → "You touch nothing. One app, one bill, done for you."

## AFTER EVERY BLOCK
"VEGA, log [N] conversations" — the scoreboard only counts what you tell it.
