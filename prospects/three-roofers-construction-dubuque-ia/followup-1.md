**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**This escalates the first touch's fact instead of restating it, and says so
outright.** Touch one told him the domain was down and framed it as a findability
problem, which is the weakest reading of it. This one names that as the small
version and makes the actual argument: a suspended page is not a neutral absence,
it is an active signal that the business has closed, and it reaches exactly the
people who were already sent to him. **Opening by correcting your own previous
email is a device worth reusing** wherever the first touch made a true observation
and drew the wrong conclusion from it, which after reading this lane is more often
than I would like.

**Ending on the asset rather than the deficiency is deliberate.** He already owns
the domain, which is the expensive and fiddly half of this for most prospects, and
the email says so. It turns a "you have a problem" email into a "you are most of
the way there" one in the last two lines, which is the same complimentary turn the
Nelson's Complete Treat draft used on 08-31.

**The fact is unusually well verified.** Montague could not load the domain
(egress-blocked), but Google's own indexed page title for that exact URL is
"Website Builder - Website Suspended," which is a crawl of the live page rather
than an AI paraphrase, and BBB, Yelp and Facebook all point to the Facebook page
as the live presence. That is a stronger read than most website-gap findings in
this repo.

carefully.** That draft argued permanence on an insurance-funded roof: the payout

same "will this roofer still exist" mechanism, one day apart, on the other roofing
prospect in this lane. Dropped on that basis alone. This draft never mentions the
