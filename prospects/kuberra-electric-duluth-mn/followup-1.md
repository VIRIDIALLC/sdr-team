**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New mechanism: the customer who hires without ever meeting you.** Every
website-track argument so far has assumed a local customer who could in principle
walk in, ask around or recognize a truck. The absentee North Shore property owner
can do none of those, so for that customer the site is not a supplement to word
of mouth, it is the entire basis of the decision. **Worth reusing in any market
with second homes, rentals or absentee owners**, which on this list also touches
resort and lake towns.

**It is asked, not asserted.** Nothing in Montague's file says Granger does cabin
or rental work, so the opener is a real question with two face-saving doors:
plenty of that work means the email is about his best customer, and none of it
means he is a local residential shop, which is a perfectly good answer and still a
reply. The North Shore having a seasonal, partly absentee ownership pattern is
general geography, not a claim about his book.

**Handles the Two Harbors problem without making it the pitch.** Montague flagged
that the business is registered at 421 Burton Road, Two Harbors, roughly 25 miles
up the shore, and warned whoever drafts not to assume a Duluth street address. The
body says "from Two Harbors down through Duluth," which is accurate, states his
service area as a strength, and avoids the trap of writing to him as though he
sits in Duluth.

**The website gap here is the most decisively verified in the batch.** A direct
WebFetch of kuberraelectric.com returned an actual DNS resolution failure
(ENOTFOUND), which is a real result rather than a blocked fetch. Nothing in the
body depends on it beyond naming what he does have, which is the Facebook page and
the BuildZoom listing, both confirmed.

**Deliberately does not repeat touch one**, which was the old pre-quality-gate
template: a generic no-website line, the 84% BrightLocal stat, an offer to put a
look together. The stat is gone and does not belong in a follow-up.

**Kept out on purpose:** the master electrician credential as the argument. It
appears in the body as one item in a list of what the page would carry, not as the
reason to build one, because "you hold a credential with nowhere to live" is the
Nelson's Complete Treat mechanism from 08-31 and I did not want a second run of
it. Also kept out: his commercial work, which would pull the email toward a
procurement argument that nothing in the file supports.

**No pricing in this one**, deliberately. Two of the five existing website
follow-ups lead into the price list, and putting it in every draft in this batch
is precisely what would make them read as one automated sequence. Here the
argument is about trust rather than cost, so the price would be answering an
objection he has not raised.
