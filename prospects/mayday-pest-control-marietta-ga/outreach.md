**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**The mechanism is the loss of visibility that comes with delegation, and it is
the opposite of every other draft in your queue.** Every one of them argues the
owner is the bottleneck. **This one argues he successfully stopped being the
bottleneck and lost the ability to see what happens on his own phones as a
result.** At seven people the calls are being answered by six other humans, and
nobody is counting the ones that are not. Genuinely new axis in this repo.

**It turns the ICP scale flag into the pitch rather than around it.** Montague's
warning is that MayDay is bigger than a clean small-shop fit. Rather than writing
a small-shop email to a fifteen-county operation and hoping he doesn't notice, the
draft opens by naming his actual size and treats it as an accomplishment. **If the
scale flag means anything, it means the small-shop email would have been the wrong
email, and this is the version that survives it.** Your call on whether MayDay is
in ICP at all, but if it is, this is the copy.

**It is also the doctrine's moat move as an opener.** Section 5 says per-client
measured numbers beat any industry stat and cannot be copied. The email quotes no
stat at all. It asks for a number he does not have and offers to produce it, which
is the whole product in one sentence.

**The forced choice is a knowledge question rather than a behavior question**,
which is why it works on an owner this size. "I know" makes him a good operator.
"I don't" is not embarrassing, because the email has already said most owners at
his size cannot. Neither door is "your shop is sloppy," and neither door is "not
interested."
