**The response-time figure held up, which is still the exception.** Rupika
saw "~3 days / 100% response rate" clearly on one search and could not
reproduce it on two follow-ups. Montague ran four independent re-checks: two

**No contradicting figure appeared anywhere.** Her call was that this is solid

For context on how rare that is: on the 08-21 batch, five out of five package
prospects had their figure fail verification, and on 08-22 one figure turned
out to belong to no real business at all. This one is clean.

**Three days is a different animal from the usual six-to-nine hours, and the
email is built on that.** Six hours is late. Three days is after the decision.
Landscaping is the trade where that matters most, because a yard redo is
discretionary and comparison-shopped, so the buyer sends the same request to
several companies in one sitting rather than calling one and waiting. The
middle paragraph is the entire argument and it is specific to his trade, not
a general speed-to-lead line.

**Why the "do you batch them" read instead of the standard opener.** A 100%
response rate next to a three-day average is not a man ignoring leads. It is
a man who never drops one and gets to them all at once, which is a real and
flattering thing to notice. Both doors stay face-saving: clearing the pile at
night means he is disciplined and busy, and "not worth stopping mid job" is a
judgment call, not a failing. Neither answer is "you're bad at this," and
"not interested" is not one of the options offered.

**It also had to not be yesterday's email.** Sierra Vista Roofing went out on
08-22 built on 100% plus six hours, same inbox, one day apart. Leading with
the 100% as praise and then pivoting to the number underneath it would have
been that draft with the trade swapped. So this one leads with the three days,
questions his method rather than his speed, and closes on an economic point
(you get paid by the patient ones) that the Sierra Vista draft does not make
anywhere.

**Scale check before you dial.** Genuinely early-stage: company established
2020, CA contractor license #1090873 active since 4/30/2022 and confirmed
active by Rupika via BuildZoom, 39 Yelp reviews, 171 photos. This is an
owner-operator, not a regional outfit, so Eric is almost certainly the person
answering. The site's /why-us page claims three generations of family
landscaping knowledge, which is worth having in your pocket for the call but
is a founder narrative rather than a verified fact, so it stayed out of the
email.

**Angle considered and dropped: the 171 photos.** He has posted 171 photos
against 39 reviews and introduces himself by name on the company Instagram.
That is a man doing his own demand generation, and doing it well. The email
would have credited the investment he already made and then asked where the
resulting quote request lands. Dropped for one reason: that is the same
mechanism as the Overson Pest Control draft from 08-22, one day apart, out of
the same inbox. It is a strong angle and I would use it on its own, but the
response-time figure here is verified and rare, and spending it to avoid a
collision I can dodge another way would be the wrong trade.
