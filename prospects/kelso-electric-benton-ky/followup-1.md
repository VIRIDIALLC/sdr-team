**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**Genuinely new mechanism for this repo: the site works on the labor side, not
the customer side.** Roughly thirty website-track emails have now been written
here and every one of them argues about customers. For a skilled trade the binding
constraint is usually not leads, it is licensed hands, and a journeyman evaluates
an employer exactly the way a homeowner evaluates a contractor, by looking the
company up. **Worth reaching for on any trade prospect who looks like they want to
grow, and it is the single best answer to the "I'm already busy" objection**,
because it takes being busy as the premise rather than arguing with it.

**It builds on the first touch without repeating it.** Touch one argued that
thirty years on the tools are invisible behind a 2020 LLC, aimed at customers.
This one applies the same underlying asymmetry to hiring, which is a different
audience, a different cost, and a different closing argument. The 2020-versus-thirty-years
fact appears in one sentence as evidence rather than as the point, and the phrase
"thirty years that nobody can see" from the first subject line is deliberately not
reused.

**It is asked, not asserted.** Nothing in Montague's file says he is hiring or
wants to. The opener is a real question with two face-saving doors: hiring through
who-you-know is what most good shops do, and not hiring at all is a fine answer
that still gets a reply. The cost claim in the last line is an argument, not a
statistic.
