**The angle is scope, and it is built on the one thing in this file that is

**I deliberately did not reference the license.** Montague found classifications
B100 (General Building Qualifier) and E200 (General Electrical Qualifier) under
license 11550016-5501, consistent across BuildZoom-derived results, but Utah
DOPL's live registry was egress-blocked and she flagged it explicitly as worth a
human check before it appears in outreach. Citing a classification we could not
verify at source is exactly the kind of claim that gets a first email dismissed
by the one person who knows it is wrong. So the email asks whether he does
construction rather than telling him he is qualified for it. The question is
better anyway: it is the same forced-choice shape as your cold-call opener, both
doors are face-saving (the construction side is real and he is bigger than his
name suggests, or it is entity housekeeping and there is nothing to defend), and
"not interested" is not one of the options offered.

**If you want to use the license on the call, verify it at DOPL first.** It
would strengthen the same argument considerably, and it is a ten-second check
you can do that we cannot from here.
