**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New mechanism: the site works after the quote, not before the call.** Every
website argument in this repo assumes the site's job is to get someone to make
contact. For a contractor who bids work the decisive moment comes later, when a
buyer who already has his number goes to check him before signing, often on behalf
of somebody who was never on the phone. **Worth reusing on any prospect who
submits proposals rather than taking service calls**, and it is another answer to
the "I don't need leads" objection, since it argues about closing work he has
already won the chance at.

**It builds on touch one's audience without repeating its argument.** The first
email asked whether the construction side is real and argued he loses the big
combined jobs because nobody can learn one outfit does both. This one takes the
same commercial buyer at a later moment and argues about verification rather than
scope. The entity-name mismatch, the Powerhouse Construction Services LLC detail
and the call-two-contractors line are all absent.

**The same-name businesses are used carefully.** Montague confirmed by DNS that
three registered Powerhouse domains belong to different companies, an Austin/San
Antonio namesake, an unrelated Ventura CA renovation contractor, and one with no
Utah connection, and that none of their indexed content mentions Vernal, the
Uintah Basin, his address or his phone. The body says a searcher finds a Texas one
and a California one, which is exactly that, and it does not claim anything about
ranking or ordering.

**Still deliberately kept out, both unchanged from the first touch:**
- **The license classifications.** B100 and E200 under license 11550016-5501 are
  BuildZoom-derived and Utah DOPL's live registry was egress-blocked. Citing a
  qualification we could not verify at source is the fastest way to be dismissed
  by the one person who knows whether it is right. **Worth ten seconds at DOPL
  before you call**, since it would strengthen this exact argument.
- **The possible Hank's Electric connection.** A "Robert Oostveen" with the normal
  two-o spelling turns up as President and Master Electrician of a separate
  established Vernal contractor. No source states they are the same man. It is
  entirely plausible in a small trade town and it is also the single worst thing
  to get wrong in writing. Good call material, not email material, and if they are
  the same person this pitch may be aimed at the wrong entity.

**What is asserted.** Only what a search returns and general truths about how
commercial buyers vet a bidder. Nothing about his revenue, his customers, his
licensing or his history appears. There is no statistic.
