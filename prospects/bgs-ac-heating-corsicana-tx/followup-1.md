**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**The angle is that his single biggest competitive advantage is the one that never
appears in the comparison.** Corsicana is roughly fifty miles from Dallas, close
enough that metro operations advertise into it and far enough that they cannot
serve it quickly. Proximity is decisive in HVAC in a Texas August and it is
invisible online, where scale reads as credibility and distance reads as nothing
at all. **Worth reusing on any prospect sitting in the shadow of a metro**, which
on this list is a recurring pattern.

reply. The eight-o'clock-window line is a general truth about how a distant
company has to dispatch, not a claim about a named competitor.
