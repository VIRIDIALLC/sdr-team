**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System. **This is the strongest
draft of the eight.**

**The mechanism is the customer who will not call you, and nothing in this repo
has argued it.** Bed bug work has a customer psychology no other trade has:
the inquiry is embarrassing, so it goes out by text or web form rather than by
phone, at night, to several companies at once, and the sender will not repeat
the request to a second company later. That makes after-hours message response
the entire ballgame for this specific service line, not a nice-to-have. It is
face-saving by construction, because nobody is awake at eleven at night and the
email says so.

**"Three hours is a fine number. It's just measured from when you woke up."**
That line is the whole email. It concedes his number is good, which is true and
disarming, and then relocates the problem somewhere the number cannot see.

**The three hour figure is asked, never asserted.** It lives on Yelp's own badge
per Rupika. Montague found nothing that reconfirms or contradicts it, so it is
the unconfirmed-but-not-contradicted bucket and the email questions it.
