**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**The mechanism is that the delay is caused by missing information rather than by
inattention, and that is new for this seat.** Every other package draft treats the
response as a message that needs sending sooner. This one says a real answer to a
quote request requires the schedule, the schedule lives back at the shop or in
somebody's head, and a man in a crawlspace has neither. **That reframes the fix
from "be faster" to "let the reply carry the calendar," which is much closer to
what the offer actually does** and which no other email in your queue says. It is
face-saving throughout: he is not ignoring anyone, he is waiting until he can give
a real answer, which is the more professional instinct.
