**The mechanism is that the advertised hours and the unreachable hours are
physically the same hours, and it is specific to this trade in a way it would not
be to any other prospect in the pipeline.** A plumber can take a call from the
truck. An electrician can take one in a crawl space. A man on a zero-turn mower in
ear protection cannot, and lawn care is close to the only trade where the entire
working day is spent inside that condition. So the eighty four hours a week they
publish as availability are eighty four hours of engine noise. The second half is
what makes it a loss rather than an inconvenience: there is no such thing as a
landscaping emergency, so unlike an HVAC or plumbing caller nobody waits, they
just move down the list, and the business never learns the call happened. That is
missed-call text-back and the AI receptionist, without naming either.

**It is face-saving by construction, and this one is unusually clean on that
point.** The premise is that he is out doing the work, which is exactly where a
small landscaping operator should be, and the copy says outright that this is not
a call he decided not to take. The doors are "most of it, that's the job" and
"I've got guys for that now," and the second one is a good outcome for us too.

**Every fact in the body is theirs and arithmetic.** Seven days a week, 7am to
7pm is their own published schedule, which Montague verified, and eighty four
hours is that multiplied out. Nothing else in the copy is a number. No rating, no
review count and no tenure figure appears.
