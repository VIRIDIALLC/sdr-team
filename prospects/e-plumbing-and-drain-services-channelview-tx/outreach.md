**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**The mechanism is that a missed call leaves no evidence, and it is new here.**
Every other draft in this repo argues about a lead that arrived and sat.
This one argues about the lead category that never registers as anything: an
unanswered call from a stranger produces no record, no name and no way to learn
from it, which is why a shop can be losing steadily and have no way to notice.
It maps about as directly onto missed-call text-back as any argument can without
naming the feature.

**It is face-saving, and the middle paragraph does that work explicitly.**
Telling a business it is invisible online is a dig unless you say plainly that
plenty of good shops run that way, so the email says it. The thin footprint is
then used as evidence for the argument rather than as a criticism: because
almost nothing about them is online, calls really are the whole funnel.

**Everything in the middle paragraph is Montague's verified finding, not a
guess.** No review count or rating surfaced anywhere, there is no BBB, Angi,
HomeAdvisor or Thumbtack presence, and the 30-plus years is their own published
claim. This is the rare case where an absence is well established, because she
went looking in eight places.

**The four hour figure is asked, never asserted.** Rupika sourced it from Yelp.
Montague could not reproduce it outside the original reference and found nothing
contradicting it, so it is the unconfirmed-but-not-contradicted bucket.
