**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**The argument is that Del Rio contains two markets running on different systems,
not that newcomers are hard to reach.** The permanent town runs on word of mouth
and this shop is presumably fine in it. The transient half cannot plug into that
network at all, is structurally unable to, and refills on a schedule. That makes it
a standing second market rather than a missed opportunity, which is what justifies
building something rather than waiting.

**Honest flag, because it matters more than usual here: this shares DNA with the
Hero AC & Heat draft from 08-31**, which argued that people moving into Casa
Grande have no access to twenty years of the owner's goodwill. Both are HVAC, both
turn on people who cannot reach an established referral network. I kept it anyway

permanent rotation, so the market never saturates and the argument is about a
system rather than a wave. And the file for this prospect is genuinely thin, which

review quote and no usable personalization detail beyond a home-address inference.
