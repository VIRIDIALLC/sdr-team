**The mechanism is that painting is bought in a race the seller cannot see.**
Every package draft this seat writes argues that a slow reply loses leads. This
one concedes that a day is not slow in any absolute sense and argues that the
unit is wrong: in a trade where the buyer has pre-committed to three bids before
sending the first message, response time is purely positional. Second is second
even if second is fast. The framing consequence, that the first painter through
the door defines the scope everyone else bids against, is the part he will
recognize immediately from his own experience of winning and losing jobs.
