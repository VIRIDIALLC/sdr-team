**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**The same-state detail is what makes this worth writing.** Generic name-collision
observations are weak, and I dropped exactly that angle for Quality Overhead Door
in this same batch as too worn. This one is different in kind: Montague found an
MGM Painting listing in **Alexandria, Virginia**, alongside the Connecticut
company that owns mgmpainting.com. A Virginia homeowner searching a Virginia
painter's name can land on a different Virginia painter with the same name and
have no reason to doubt it. That is a specific, checkable, materially worse
problem than sharing a name with a company two states away.

**Deliberately does not repeat touch one**, which was a genuinely good email: it
used his eleven years running a sporting-goods store to argue that he already
knows what a storefront does, and that he has the reputation but not the window.
That biography does not appear here at all, and neither does the storefront
metaphor, the 4.5-star Angi rating, or the Nextdoor recommendations.

**The frame is "address problem, not website problem,"** which is doing real work.
It sidesteps the objection a man who has ignored one website email is most likely
holding, that he does not need a website, by agreeing with the spirit of it and
arguing for something narrower. Ending on the fact that the obvious Virginia
domains are still available makes the next step feel small and specific rather
than like a project.

**What is asserted and how confident it is.** Montague DNS-checked seven guessed
domains for him, all NXDOMAIN, resolver sanity-checked against known-good domains,
which is decisive: he has no site and the natural Virginia versions of the name
are genuinely unregistered. The two collision businesses were confirmed as
different companies by their own contact pages. The one soft edge is that the
Alexandria listing was found on HomeAdvisor and everything this run was
search-snippet-sourced rather than page-rendered, so the body says "another
listing under that name over in Alexandria," which is accurate to what was
actually found, rather than claiming a rival company operates there.

**Do not attach either collision domain to this record**: `mgmpaintingco.com` is
Fort Collins, Colorado, and `mgmpainting.com` is Bridgeport, Connecticut. Neither
