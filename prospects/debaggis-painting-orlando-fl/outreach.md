voicemail.** The lead arrives carrying someone else's trust, which is why it closes
so well, and that same borrowed trust is why it does not survive a missed call:
the caller has no relationship with Jim to draw on, so there is no reason for a
second attempt. **This is an argument about how the lead arrived rather than about
Jim, his trade, or his hours**, which is what makes it the third painting mechanism
in your queue without colliding with the other two.
