# Ad-management retainer facts (source of truth for Elly, track: ads)

Real numbers only — pulled verbatim from viridiaanalytics.com's own
published case studies and pricing/ad-campaigns pages on 2026-08-04. If
those pages change, update here first; Elly pitches from this file, not
from memory.

## The angle

Kevin and his team are former Yelp employees and a Yelp Advertising
Premier Partner Agency — real platform insiders, not a generic agency
guessing at how Yelp's ad auction works. The pitch is: you're already on
Yelp, this makes sure you're not leaving leads on the table there.

## The retainer

10% of the client's total ad spend that Kevin's team is responsible for
managing. Not a flat fee — scales with their real budget. Ad management
can be added on top of any of the CRM/automation packages, or run on its
own; don't quote a flat dollar number, this is a conversation for Kevin
or his AE once a prospect replies.

## Real proof points (use these, don't paraphrase the numbers)

**TFP Landscaping** (Phoenix landscaper, 18 yrs in business, Business
Essentials package):
- 284% more leads, same ad spend
- 242 total leads in 30 days (179 from Yelp Ads)
- 39.95% close rate on AI-booked estimates
- 28 estimates booked end-to-end by AI inside Yelp's Request a Quote flow
- Response time went from 4+ hours to instant

**Finish Coat Painting** (residential/commercial painting, Never Miss a
Lead package):
- 29 AI-booked estimates in 30 days
- $15.05k in closed-won revenue from those AI-booked estimates alone
  (does not include phone/website leads on top of that)
- 138 total leads in 30 days (81 from Yelp Ads)

## What NOT to do

Don't invent a lead count, close rate, or dollar figure for the prospect
being pitched — the numbers above are Kevin's real, already-closed
clients, cited as proof of what the system can do, not a promise of what
this specific prospect will get.
