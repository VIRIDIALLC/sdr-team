**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**The response time is asked as "hours," never as a number, and that is
deliberate.** Montague reconfirmed roughly 4 hours on Yelp's "Pest Control"
category tag specifically, but the same listing shows anywhere from 50 minutes
to 2 days across its other category tags. Her explicit instruction was to use it
as a loose honest signal, not a stat to quote. So the body says "hours" and
offers him a third door, that Yelp is measuring something odd, which is very
possibly true and costs nothing.

**The angle is urgency decay, which is specific to this trade.** Every other
prospect in today's batch loses jobs to a competitor who replied first. Pest is
different: the customer is upset right now, the willingness to wait is measured
in minutes rather than days, and it decays on its own whether or not a
competitor shows up. That reframes the pitch away from "you're slow" and toward
"the clock on this trade is shorter than the clock on any other." It is also
complimentary by construction, because the argument concedes that his price and
his reviews are not the problem.

**"Under a house doing an inspection" is grounded**, not color. Reviews
repeatedly describe Jesse personally doing the inspections, which is exactly why
the phone goes unanswered. It names the reason without making it a failing.

**What the body asserts.** That his Yelp shows hours on quote requests (asked,
not asserted) and that he does inspections himself (from his reviews). The pest
list (mice, wasps) comes from his own service list.

**Before you dial, and this matters:** BBB rates them **F**, not accredited,
with an accreditation-revocation alert and a licensing alert on file for
"failure to have a required competency license." That is a real BBB record, not
noise. Montague's read, which I agree with, is that it looks like a
stretched-thin owner-operator behind on back office rather than a bad actor, and

email copy and should not be raised on the call unless he raises it.** Know it
before you dial in case he does.
