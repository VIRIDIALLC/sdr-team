**This is the best-supported hook of today's seven.** Their own marketing says
"staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week" and "on-site within an hour, any hour of
the day." Yelp's message response time reads about a day at an 85% response

**The escape hatch in the opener is deliberate and honest.** "Or is Yelp just
counting something different" is a real possibility, not politeness. On-site
dispatch speed and Yelp message response are genuinely different metrics, and
their reviews do cite within-the-hour arrival once contacted. Offering her that
door costs nothing and makes the email hard to be annoyed by, and if she takes
it she has still answered.

the emergency lane outright, which is the half she is proud of, and isolates the
non-emergency quote request as a structurally different animal: patient-looking,
comparison-shopped, and lost silently. That framing means nothing in the email
says she is failing at the thing her company is built around.
