**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New mechanism for this track: the site as a filter, not a magnet.** Every
website-track email this team has written argues that a site brings more people
in. This one argues it keeps the wrong people out, which is a different promise
and lands better on an owner-operator whose real constraint is his own hours
rather than his phone volume. Painting is the right trade for it because the
estimate genuinely costs him a truck trip and an hour, unlike a trade that quotes
over the phone. **Worth reusing anywhere the prospect is the person doing the
work and the estimate requires a site visit.**

**The question has two face-saving doors and is a real question.** A high close
rate means he's good and the site only gets him more at-bats. A low one means
he's driving for free, which is a shared contractor grievance with no shame in
it. "Not interested" is not one of the offered answers.

**Deliberately does not repeat touch one**, which was the old pre-quality-gate
template: a generic no-website observation plus the 84% BrightLocal stat plus an
offer to put a look together. **Every one of the seven 08-17 website first touches
was that same email with the nouns swapped**, so anything specific is an
improvement, but this one deliberately avoids the two follow-up mechanisms
already used in this lane: the ran-the-search-and-you-weren't-in-it opener (A+
Garage Doors 08-21) and the your-photos-are-trapped-on-Facebook opener (RidgeTop
08-21). Montague did name two Rapid City competitors with real sites, Inside Out
