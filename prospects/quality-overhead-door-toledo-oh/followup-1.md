budget is a dial you can move in an afternoon. Organic ranking and reviews are
not. Every other channel this business has takes months to change and the demand

specific to the trade rather than a general "you should advertise" argument.
Sending it in early September is deliberate: it lands before the season rather
than during it, and the closing line says so.
