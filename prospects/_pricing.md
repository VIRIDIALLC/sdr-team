update here first — Sage quotes from this file, not from memory.

## How to match scope to a tier

Match on page count and feature list, in that order. If a prospect's
stated needs land cleanly within one tier's page count and don't require
a feature from a higher tier, quote that tier. If they explicitly ask for
something not listed anywhere above (e-commerce/online sales, member
logins, bookings/scheduling integrations, multi-language, anything
custom-sounding), that's a `needs_kevin` case — don't stretch Premium to
cover it and don't invent a price above $1,200+.
