**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New mechanism: the selling season and the working season are different.** The
argument is that visibility and demand are out of phase for a seasonal trade, so
the months when he has the most presence on the street are not the months when the
big jobs are chosen. That is a different claim from the seasonal argument already
used in this lane, and see the dropped-angle block for why the distinction
matters.

agreement. Same device as the H&J close in this batch, though the question there
is factual and this one is about his own trade, where he is the expert and I am
not.

**Angle considered and dropped:** the winterization/off-season services timing
argument, in the shape the High Country Lawn & Landscape follow-up used on 08-21:
build it now so search engines have found it before the cold-weather work starts.

Dropped deliberately, and the distinction is worth keeping. High Country's version
is about *service seasonality*, that certain jobs only get searched for in a
narrow window, so the site has to exist before the window opens. This draft's
version is about *decision timing*, that the purchase is chosen months before the
work happens and in a season when he is invisible. They sound adjacent and they
are not the same argument, but they are close enough that writing High Country's
version again for a second landscaper would have been the collision. This one
argues about when the customer makes up their mind, and never mentions search at
all.
