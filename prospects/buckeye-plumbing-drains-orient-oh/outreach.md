**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**The mechanism is volume, not speed, and that is new for this seat.** Every
package draft in this repo argues about how FAST a lead gets answered. This one
argues about HOW MANY, and it never says he is slow. 166 requests is the largest
inbound figure any prospect has carried here, and the argument is that a number
that size stops being a discipline question and becomes an arithmetic one: the
replies have to come out of billable hours, and he does not have enough of them.
That reframing is the entire email, and it is complimentary by construction,
because a 166-request listing is evidence his marketing works.
