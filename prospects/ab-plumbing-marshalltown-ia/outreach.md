**Note for Kevin:** Website track.

**The whole email rests on one fact and it is unusually well established.**
Rupika flagged the name collision at sourcing and Montague confirmed it
independently: unrelated AB Plumbings in Round Lake IL, Lakewood OH ("AB
Plumbing and Rooter"), Columbus OH, Tucson AZ and Hamilton Township NJ, plus
abplumbing.net belonging to a Sacramento/Bay Area business. That is five
other businesses across four states, which is what the subject line and the
opening sentence are counting. Everything in the body is checkable by them
on their own phone in about ten seconds, which is the property I want most
in a cold email.

**Why the name collision and not the usual "you don't have a website" line.**
This track has exactly one natural hook and it is the hook most at risk of
producing the same email every time. I flagged that as a standing rule on
08-18 after the RidgeTop draft nearly repeated E-Z Garage Door word for
word. Here the collision converts the generic argument into a specific one:
they don't need a website explained to them as marketing, they need to be
told that the internet currently has no way to tell them apart from five
strangers. A site stops being a brochure and starts being the thing that
makes them the AB Plumbing in Marshalltown.

**I deliberately left out the 84% BrightLocal stat**, the standard
website-track data point. The argument is already concrete without it, and
that stat is the single element that makes every website draft in this repo
resemble the last one. Same call I made on MGM Painting on 08-21. If you
want it back in, it slots as one sentence after the second paragraph: "84%
of people looking for a local business searched online in the last three
months, and right now they're not finding you." Worth noting the persona
file itself says to recheck that figure is current before reusing it, and I
have not, which is a second reason it stayed out.

**The website gap itself: CONFIRMED, with one honest hole.** No dedicated
domain surfaced across multiple search phrasings including phone-anchored
queries. No BBB profile. Bizapedia confirms an Iowa LLC exists but the
officer detail is paywalled. The one source Montague could not check is Iowa
Secretary of State's own filing, blocked by her session's egress proxy. Every
other channel agrees there is no site.
