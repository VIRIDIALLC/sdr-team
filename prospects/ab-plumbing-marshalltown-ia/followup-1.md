**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New mechanism, and I think it's the most reusable one to come out of this
batch: the site's job is to update the customers you already have.** Every
website argument in this repo, without exception, has been about strangers,
whether finding you, believing you, or telling you apart. This one is about the
people who already hired you and like you, and who are carrying a stale picture of
what you sell. For a small trade business those people are the cheapest revenue
available and the easiest to reach, and nothing currently reaches them. **Worth
reaching for whenever a prospect has added a service line, changed what they
cover, or expanded their area.**

**It also sidesteps the objection this prospect is most likely holding.** A shop
that ignored a website email once is usually thinking "I get enough work by word
of mouth." This email agrees with that and then points out that word of mouth
transmits what you did for someone, not what you do now.

**Deliberately does not repeat touch one.** The name-collision argument, five
other AB Plumbings across four states, was the entire first email and it is
referenced in one clause purely as a handoff before dropping it completely.

the same May 2026 Facebook post about the new sewer service, but the mechanism was
that a Facebook post disappears a day after it goes up, which is the RidgeTop
Roofing follow-up mechanism from 08-21. This draft never mentions Facebook or how
long a post lasts. The mechanism here is that a past customer's idea of you goes
stale, which is a different problem with a different fix. **Same fact, different
