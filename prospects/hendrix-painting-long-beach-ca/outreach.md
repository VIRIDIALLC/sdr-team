**Note for Kevin:** Package track.

**Eight hours is the longest response figure in this batch and it is a full
working day, which is what the subject line and the first door are built on.**
Six hours reads as late. Eight hours means the request sits from the moment the
truck leaves until it comes back, and for a thirty-year painting company that
is not negligence, it is the job. The first door says exactly that, so the
question opens by handing him the reasonable explanation rather than making him
find it.

**The argument in the second paragraph is specific to painting and I have not
used it anywhere.** A painting quote is almost never a price over email, it is
a walkthrough. So the first reply is not a number, it is a calendar slot, and
the painter who books that slot has the homeowner's Saturday morning and a
face-to-face before anyone else has spoken to them. That reframes speed as
calendar position rather than as speed for its own sake, and it makes the loss
concrete without quoting a stat or naming a single feature of the system.
