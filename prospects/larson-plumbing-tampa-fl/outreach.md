**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**The mechanism is his own published promise, and the twist is the good part.**
24 hour emergency service is a claim he made, not an assumption I made about him,
so the opener is grounded in something he can check in one click. Then the email
does something no other draft in this repo does: **it explicitly rejects the
after-hours emergency argument and says the real loss is the non-urgent
after-hours lead.** That is true, it is counterintuitive, and it is the sort of
thing that makes an owner stop on the third line.

**That twist is also what keeps it clear of Anderson Restoration.** Anderson
(09-11) is the 2am argument: an emergency caller takes whoever answers. This email
names that scenario and sets it aside. Two drafts in the same queue, opposite
conclusions from the same starting point, which is about as clean a separation as
same-adjacent mechanisms get.

**Vonda is in the email and I thought hard about that.** She is confirmed on their
own About page as his wife and the business's Administrative Assistant and Sales
Rep, who joined once their two kids were grown. Naming her says I actually read
about the company. The line is "you or Vonda, and there are only so many nights,"
which is a statement about arithmetic, not about her. **If it reads wrong to you,
cut "or Vonda" and the sentence still works.**

**"I'd bet you keep that more often than not" is a compliment doing real work.**
It concedes he is good at this before raising the gap, so there is no defensive
exit. The email never once says he is dropping calls.

**No response-time figure exists and none was invented.** Family-owned technique,
no Yelp signal. Persona's underlying-problem fallback, same as all ten today.

**Before you dial:** phone **(813) 242-0911**, identical on BBB and their own
site. Founded 6/15/1991, started with one van. Single Tampa location. He comes
