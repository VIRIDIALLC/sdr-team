**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New purpose for the site in this repo: protecting price rather than generating
leads.** Every website argument here has been about getting in front of more
people or being believed by them. This one says the site's job is to keep him from
being compared on a single number, which is the actual failure mode for any shop
that has deliberately built a higher cost base. It also answers the objection a
busy twelve-year shop is most likely holding, that he does not need more calls,
by agreeing and arguing about margin instead.

**The union fact is finally used, and used differently than it would have been.**
Montague surfaced "union technicians only" and I kept it out of the first touch on
08-31 because the obvious use of it, a credential with nowhere to live, was the
Kelso Electric legitimacy mechanism from two days earlier. Here it is not a
credential at all, it is a cost structure, and the argument runs from the cost
rather than from the prestige. **Worth keeping as a general move: a fact rejected
for the mechanism it usually implies can often be reused on a different mechanism
entirely.** Same lesson as the AB Plumbing sewer-line fact in this batch.

Garage Door follow-up from 08-21, which argued that "most affordable in the Pine

price, this one about justifying a high one. I called mirror-image pairs a
template risk when I dropped the Kuberra domain angle earlier in this batch, so

share no sentence, and the arguments run in opposite directions rather than being
