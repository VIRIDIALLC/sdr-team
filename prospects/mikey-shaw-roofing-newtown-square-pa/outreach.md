**The mechanism is two revenue lines competing for the same hours.** Holiday
lighting is on his own service list alongside roofing, repairs, siding, gutters
and 24/7 emergency. A roofer who also hangs Christmas lights has a hard, dated

October to December crunch he is being asked about starts in about three weeks,

different and much weaker email.
