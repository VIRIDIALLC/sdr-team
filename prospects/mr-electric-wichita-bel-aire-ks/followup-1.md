**Note for Kevin:** Ads track, second touch.

**This turns the franchise flag into the email instead of hiding it.** The first
touch deliberately said nothing about Mr. Electric being a Neighborly franchise,
because Montague found corporate does fund general "digital ads" for franchisees

**Why it works as an ads argument.** National brand spend and a local auction are
genuinely different purchases. Brand money buys awareness of the name; the
auction is decided per ZIP, per day, at a price somebody has to actually choose.
That is a structural fact about how the product is bought, not a claim about
Neighborly's program, and it is the same reason a franchisee can be well
supported nationally and still invisible on the search that matters in Bel Aire.
Both doors are face-saving: corporate does bid, in which case he says so and
we're done, or nobody does, which is not his fault.
