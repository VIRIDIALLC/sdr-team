**Note for Kevin:** Package track.

**I overrode Montague's CALL CARD hook, and you should know why before you
call, because her underlying finding is genuinely interesting.** Her hook
is the discrepancy she found: the site claims "over two decades" in
business, while Blue Book lists the entity as established 2020. That is a

It is the wrong opener for a cold email. Leading with "your marketing
claim doesn't match your corporate registration" asks a stranger to
account for himself in the first two lines. Neither door is face-saving:
one answer is an innocent explanation he now owes you, the other is that
he's overstating his experience. That's the opposite of the two
face-saving doors the opener rule is built on. **Keep it in your pocket for
a live call once there's rapport, where "so what's the 2020 story, did you
take it over?" is a friendly question. It just can't be the first thing he
reads from a stranger.**

24/7 is their own published marketing claim, so the tension between the

which is genuinely impressive and opens the conversation about the ones he
can't take, or it rolls to voicemail, which is honest and normal. It's a
question. And it's specific to a business that chose to advertise 24/7
rather than a gap any shop has.
