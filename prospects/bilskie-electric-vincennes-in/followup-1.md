**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New mechanism, and the best one to come out of the back half of this batch:
demand that never forms.** Every argument in this repo, mine included, assumes a
customer exists and the question is whether they reach you, believe you, or pick
you. This one says a category of work is never commissioned at all, because the
homeowner cannot estimate the downside and will not invite somebody in to find
out. Those jobs never appear as a lost bid because they never became a bid. **This
is the strongest available answer to "I'm busy enough," since it does not ask him
to take work from anyone**, and it is specific to electrical, where fear of what is
behind the wall is the dominant emotion and the reason people put projects off for
years.

**Deliberately does not repeat touch one**, which was entirely about his name
landing on HomeAdvisor and a quoted review using the word "found." None of that is
here: no HomeAdvisor, no review quote, no directory listings, no argument about
