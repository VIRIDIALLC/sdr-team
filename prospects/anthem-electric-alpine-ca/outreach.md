**The angle is the front door against the back door, and it is the best thing in
this file.** Montague flagged that the positive sentiment about Kenneth's
responsiveness comes from customers describing jobs he had already booked, which
is a completely different channel from an inbound Yelp quote request. That is
usually written down as a caution. Read the other way it is the whole email:
once you are a customer of Anthem Electric you get a responsive electrician, and
before you are a customer you are in a line, and both of those are true at the
same time. It is the most face-saving frame available, because it says his
service is genuinely good and the gap is purely structural at the point of
intake. It also happens to be exactly what The Complete System fixes.

I have not seen this mechanism used in this repo before. Nearest neighbors are
KJC 08-28 (two kinds of lead sitting in one queue) and Passionate Plumber 08-26
(the percentage who never hear back), and both of those are about the queue
itself. This one is about two different populations getting two different
companies.
