**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**This is the inverted draft of the batch, and the mechanism is genuinely new
for this seat: follow-up, not speed.** Katy Pro is the rare prospect whose
response time is actually good and independently corroborated. Montague traced
the "came by for a consultation within a couple hours" review text and found it
describes the same fast-response reputation as the Yelp metric rather than
contradicting it, and a separate search returned "responds in about 2 hours"
against Rupika's 3. That is a real 2-to-3 hour range, not instability.

So the standing package opener would have been an insult here. Every other draft
in this repo argues some version of "you are slow." This one concedes in the
second line that he is fast, which is both true and the most disarming thing we
can say, and then argues that speed wins the first conversation while
persistence wins the job. That maps directly onto the Velocify evidence in
`_acquisition-doctrine.md` (93% of conversions land by the sixth attempt), which
is Tier 1 and which we sell. **The stat is deliberately not quoted in the body**
— the argument is stronger as his own arithmetic than as a cited number, and
short bodies are the standing shape.

**Reusable principle, and this is the second time it has earned its keep:**
against an operator who is already good at the thing we sell, find the question
their own competence proves they are asking and cannot answer. Same move as
Green Dreams on 09-04, applied to a different competence.
