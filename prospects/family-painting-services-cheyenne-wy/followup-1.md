**Note for Kevin:** Website track, second touch.

**New mechanism: the site as the destination for advertising she already does
offline.** Every website-track argument in this repo so far has been about online
discovery, whether being found, being believed, or being compared. This one says
the site's job is to catch demand created in the physical world, and residential
painting is the perfect trade for it because the vehicle parks in one driveway on
one residential street for days at a time. It is complimentary rather than
corrective, it costs her nothing to verify, and no template could produce it.

**It also makes the Ueniweb point land without insulting her.** The free-builder
subdomain is the most concrete thing in her file, and the obvious way to use it
is "a free page makes you look unprofessional," which is a dig at how she looks
and is the Kelso 08-26 legitimacy mechanism besides. Reframing it as *the URL
does not physically fit on a truck door* makes the same point as a practical
problem rather than a judgment. **Worth keeping: when the sharp fact is
embarrassing to the prospect, find the version where it is an inconvenience
instead of a failing.**

ueniweb dot com") rather than as a link, so it reads the way she would say it out
loud, which is the whole point of the sentence, and so no mail client turns it
into a live link to a competitor's page.
