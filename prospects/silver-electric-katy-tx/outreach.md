**Note for Kevin:** Package track. The Complete System.

**The mechanism is demand concentration, and it is new for this seat.** Every
other draft in this repo treats leads as arriving at a steady rate, so a
four hour average is either fine or not fine. This one argues the average is the
wrong unit: generator revenue in the Houston metro arrives almost entirely in
the days after a regional outage, which is precisely when everyone else is
messaging too and when a four hour reply is late rather than normal. His own
number is not disputed anywhere in the email. What is disputed is what it is
measured over.

**It is face-saving by construction and that is the point of the last
paragraph.** Nobody staffs an office for a surge that happens twice a year. That
is not a failure, it is arithmetic, and it is the single clearest case for
automation over hiring that this ICP has. It is also the closest thing in the
batch to a natural bridge into the offer without naming a feature.

**Generator and EV charger installs are verified service lines**, from
Montague's fetch of the live site (panel upgrades, rewiring, EV chargers,
generators). The outage framing is not invented for the email, it rests on the
service line they actually publish and on Houston being Houston. No specific
storm is named, because naming one would be a claim about their business I
cannot source.
