**The mechanism is that one missed call is a lost job and another is a lost
channel.** A realtor sending WDO inspections is a recurring referral source with a
hard deadline attached to every single request, so the cost of missing her is not
one inspection, it is every inspection she would have sent afterwards. The
homeowner, by contrast, has a problem that does not resolve itself and will try
