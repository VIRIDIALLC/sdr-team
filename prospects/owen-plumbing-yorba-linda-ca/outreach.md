**What it is built on instead: the two kinds of customer on the same phone.**
The one verified, load-bearing fact is that this is a referral shop. Founded
1999 by Scott and his wife as a referral business per their own /about copy,
and independently corroborated by multiple Yelp reviews from customers who
say they have used him fifteen and twenty years. A referral and a Yelp quote
request are the same phone and completely different clocks. The referral
already chose him before dialing and will wait, and he finds out if that
patience runs out. The Yelp stranger chose three plumbers at once and is
choosing again within the hour, and Scott never learns that one existed.
**The silent loss is the argument, not slowness.** A twenty-seven-year
referral shop has never needed a system for the customer who leaves without
saying anything, because until Yelp there wasn't one.

**Both doors are face-saving and neither says he is bad at this.** Door one is
that he gets to the box between jobs, which means he is working. Door two is
a deliberate strategic read, that the regulars are the business and Yelp is
upside. A real owner can pick either without defending himself, and "not
interested" is not one of the options offered.
